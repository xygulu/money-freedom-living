The phrase "owning your mistakes" might sting a little. Because in your experience, admitting fault meant getting scolded, meant bowing your head, meant "you're not good enough." So you learned to argue, to shift the blame outward, to tough it out. But have you noticed — the less willing you are to admit something, the heavier it gets? True ownership isn't pressing yourself down; it's setting down what's been weighing on your heart. The moment you admit it, you might panic, might feel terrible about yourself — but afterward, you actually stand more steady. Because you no longer have to strain to guard that "I'm fine." In this stage we start with very small mistakes; we won't touch what you can't carry yet. What you're here to experience isn't guilt — it's the exhale of letting go.
