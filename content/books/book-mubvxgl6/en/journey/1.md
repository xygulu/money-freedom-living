Have you noticed how rarely you stop? One thing after another, you fill yourself up with busyness, as if the moment you paused, something would catch up with you. Yet that vague tiredness, that quiet resentment in your heart — it never disappears once the busy work is done. Life, in the end, comes down to one thing: seeing yourself clearly. Not fixing yourself, not scolding yourself — simply seeing first. In this stage, we're in no hurry to change anything. There's only one thing to do: slow down, and look back at what you've been doing, hiding from, and waiting for. Those uncomfortable moments aren't problems — they're clues. You don't need to figure it all out right away. You only need to dare to take one look. Seeing it is already the beginning.
