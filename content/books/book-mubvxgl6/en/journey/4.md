You've been seeing, mirroring, and owning — now comes the step most easily skipped: allowing yourself to live well. You may have already sensed it: seeing yourself isn't the hard part. What's hard is, after seeing, allowing yourself to be treated well. When someone is kind to you, your instinct is to wave it away; when it's your turn to enjoy something, a voice inside says "not yet deserved." We've met that voice now. We recognize it. And you don't have to obey it anymore. Living well for yourself isn't selfish, and it isn't a reward you must earn — it's the way you were meant to live all along. This stage has no grand moves, only small things: accepting a compliment, leaving some blank space, allowing one unnecessary splurge and one purchase without guilt. Little by little, replace "I don't deserve it" with "I'm allowed." Life has only one thing, and in the end it comes down to this: live well for yourself.
